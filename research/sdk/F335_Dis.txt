2.1. - Removing Covers

   Removing the F-335 covers is made easy by a locking system, that eliminates the need for all but
   three screws.
1. Turn off power on scanner.
2. Disconnect all cables from scanner.
3. Remove film catch basket from side of scanner.
4. Gently pull on the side of the scanner. There are three locks that will snap out of place.

Attention: Do not touch anything inside the scanner while the power is on. The cover is only off to
allow dust and debris to be blown out of the scanner.

WARNUNG: Berhren Sie keine Bauteile innerhalb des Scanners, whrend das Gert eingeschaltet ist. Die
Abdeckung wird nur deshalb abgenommen, damit Staub und Verschmutzungen aus dem Inneren des
Scanners heraus geblasen werden kˆnnen.

ATTENTION: ne touchez aucun element interne du scanner lorsque ce dernier est sous
tension. Le couvercle est ouvert pour permettre deliminer poussiere et debris du scanner.

2.8. - Blower Assembly
1.   Turn the scanner off.
2.   Unplug the USB and power cables from the back of the scanner unit.
3.   Remove all three covers (see 2.1 page 4).
4.   Locate the Fan filter. It is located on the back of the scanner, just under the plastic fan cover.




Pakon F-335 Dissembly Manual                         8/5/05                                          14
5. Pull straight back on the plastic fan cover until it comes free from the scanner housing.




6. Remove the filter from inside the cover.




7. Unscrew the eight (Phillips 3” No. 2 Bit) screws holding the blower assembly to scanner frame.
   Note: Blower Motor is held to the Scanner chassis by double sided tape.
8. Separate Blower Motor from assembly brackets.
9. Proceed to next Section (Power Supply) to remove power cable or cut.

2.14. - Film Path (Transport) Assembly
1.  Turn the scanner off.
2.  Unplug the USB and power cables from the back of the scanner unit.
3.  Remove all three covers (see 2.1 page 4).
4.  Turn scanner onto the exposed side.
5.  Unscrew the six screws holding the bottom plate in place.
6.  Remove the bottom plate from scanner.
7.  Disconnect all film path assembly cables from the motor control board if they have not already been
    removed.
8. Turn the scanner into its proper orientation (front facing technician).
9. Ensure the Entrance Film Ramp (2.2 pg 8) and Light Shield (2.3 pg 8) have been removed from the
    scanner unit. before proceeding to the next step.
10. . Remove one (Hex 7/64”) screw and washer mounting the ground straps to the scanner chassis.
11. Cut the three cable straps located on the wiring harness let to the Film Path (transport) assembly.
12. Unscrew the four (Hex 7/64”) screws holding the Film Path assembly on the scanner frame.
13. Remove the Film Path (Transport) assembly from the scanner unit.



Pakon F-335 Dissembly Manual                    8/5/05                                       21
14. Unscrew the Six (Hex 3/32”) screws holding the Transport motors to the Film Path assembly.
15. Remove the Transport motors from the Film Path (Transport) assembly.




2.15. - Light Cavity Assembly
1.   Turn the scanner off.
2.   Unplug the USB and power cables from the back of the scanner unit.
3.   Remove all three covers (see 2.1 page 4).
4.   Turn scanner onto the left exposed side.
5.   Unscrew the four (Hex 2.5 mm) screws holding the bottom plate in place.
6.   Unscrew the two (Phillips 3” No. 2 Bit) screws holding the bottom plate in place.
7.   Remove the bottom plate from the scanner unit.
8.   Ensure the Entrance Film Ramp (2.2 pg 8), Light Shield (2.3 pg 8), Film Path Assembly (2.14 pg 21)
     have been removed from the scanner unit before proceeding to the next step.
