When to recalibrate

References are tied to one scanner configuration. Change resolution, integration time or lamp drive and they stop applying — the affected cards below turn amber and scanning will offer to re-run them. Banding or colour casts in scans are the classic symptom of a stale flat field.

Calibration references are per-unit and per-configuration · stored with the app, backed up on disk

Why this matters

Every pixel you see is (raw − dark) × gain before any colour work. Good references are the difference between a clean scan and one with banding baked into every frame.
